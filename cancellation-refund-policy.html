Cancellation & Refund Policy

Our rules for cancelling an order, requesting a return, reporting a problem and receiving an approved refund.

Effective 31 August 2026

You may request cancellation before dispatch. Eligible return requests must be raised within 7 calendar days after delivery. Faulty, damaged, incorrect or materially misdescribed items are handled fairly under this policy and applicable law.

1. Cancelling an order

  • Contact us as soon as possible with the order number.
  • An order can be cancelled without a cancellation charge if it has not been dispatched or sent for requested customisation.
  • Once dispatched, cancellation may not be possible. You may refuse delivery only after contacting us, or request a return after delivery if eligible.
  • If MudduGumma cancels an order because of stock, payment, serviceability or another fulfilment issue, any captured payment will be refunded.

2. Return request window

Contact us within 7 calendar days of recorded delivery. Requests received after this period may be declined unless applicable law requires a remedy or we agree otherwise in writing.

3. Return eligibility

For a standard return, the product must be unused, unworn, unwashed, unaltered, undamaged by the customer and returned with original tags, accessories, blouse piece (where supplied), packaging and invoice/order proof.

A return, replacement or other appropriate remedy may be available where the product is damaged in transit, defective, incorrect, incomplete or materially different from its website description. Contact us promptly and provide clear photographs and the order number so we can assist.

Natural saree variations: Minor weave irregularities, thread movement, texture differences and reasonable colour differences caused by screens or lighting may be characteristics of handcrafted or woven products and are not automatically defects.

4. Non-returnable items

Unless damaged, defective, incorrect or otherwise protected by applicable law, we cannot accept returns for:

  • Sarees or garments altered, cut, stitched, washed, worn or damaged after delivery.
  • Items customised at the customer's request, including fall/pico work, blouse stitching or other personal alterations.
  • Products missing original tags, included pieces, accessories or packaging.
  • Gift cards or items clearly marked final sale/non-returnable before purchase.
  • Returns showing perfume, makeup, sweat, stains, pet hair or other signs of use.

5. How to request a cancellation or return

  1. Email or call us using the details below.
  2. Provide the order number, item name, reason and photographs where the item is damaged, defective, wrong or incomplete.
  3. Wait for return approval and the return instructions. Do not send an item without confirmation, because the correct return destination and courier steps may vary.
  4. Pack the complete item securely and retain the courier receipt/tracking until the request is resolved.

6. Return shipping and inspection

For an approved damaged, defective, incorrect or incomplete order, MudduGumma will arrange or reimburse reasonable return shipping as communicated. For an approved preference/change-of-mind return, the customer may be responsible for return shipping.

Returned products are inspected after receipt. If the item does not meet the eligibility conditions, we will explain the reason and may return it to the customer, subject to applicable law and delivery charges.

7. Refund method and timing

After an eligible return is received and approved, we will initiate the refund to the original payment method within 7–10 business days. Banks and payment providers may take additional time to credit the amount.

For approved cancellations before dispatch, the refund will be initiated after the cancellation is confirmed. Original shipping charges may be non-refundable for preference-based returns. Where we supplied a damaged, defective, wrong or incomplete item, the appropriate approved refund will include amounts required by applicable law.

If a refund appears delayed after the stated processing period, contact us with the order number and payment reference so we can trace it with the payment provider.
