Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal information MudduGumma collects, why we use it, when we share it and the choices available to you.

MudduGumma uses personal information only for legitimate store operations, customer service, legal compliance and communications you choose to receive. We do not sell your personal information.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies when you visit the MudduGumma website, create or use an account, place an order, join a notification list, contact us, use the cart or wishlist, or otherwise interact with our online store.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Name, email address, mobile number and account login information.
  • Delivery and billing address, order details, chosen products and customer-service messages.
  • Preferences such as wishlist, cart selections, size or colour choices.
  • Information you voluntarily provide through email, telephone, WhatsApp, Instagram or website forms.

Information collected automatically

We may receive device, browser, IP address, page interaction, error, referral and approximate location information through server logs or security/analytics technologies. The website also uses browser storage to keep cart and wishlist selections.

3. How we use information

  • To create and secure customer accounts.
  • To process, confirm, deliver, support, cancel, return or refund orders.
  • To communicate order status, respond to enquiries and prevent fraud or misuse.
  • To operate, troubleshoot, measure and improve the website and product selection.
  • To send offers or new-arrival messages only where allowed and where you have chosen to receive them.
  • To comply with tax, accounting, payment, legal and regulatory duties.

4. When information is shared

We may share only the information reasonably required with:

  • Payment processors such as Razorpay and participating banks/payment networks.
  • Courier, logistics, address-verification and delivery partners.
  • Website hosting, cloud, email, security, analytics and customer-support providers acting for us.
  • Professional advisers, regulators, law-enforcement or authorities where required by law or to protect rights, safety and security.
  • A successor in a lawful business transfer, subject to appropriate confidentiality and legal safeguards.

These organisations may have their own privacy notices for activities they control independently.

5. Payment information

Online payments are handled through the payment option shown at checkout. MudduGumma does not directly store your complete card number, CVV, UPI PIN, banking password or OTP. Payment processors may collect and use payment, fraud-prevention and transaction information under their own privacy terms and applicable regulations.

6. Retention and security

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for orders, support, fraud prevention, legal claims, tax/accounting duties or another disclosed purpose. Retention periods vary by the type of record and applicable law.

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, including HTTPS and access controls. No internet system can be guaranteed completely secure, so please use a strong password and never share OTPs or payment credentials.

7. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law and necessary identity verification, you may ask to access, correct or delete personal information, withdraw optional consent, or stop promotional communications. Certain order and financial records may need to be retained by law.

You can clear browser-stored cart or wishlist information through your browser settings. You may unsubscribe from optional marketing through the method provided in that message or by contacting us.

Our store is intended for adults and customers able to contract under applicable law. We do not knowingly seek personal information directly from children.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy to reflect operational, legal or technology changes. The latest version will appear here with a revised effective date.
