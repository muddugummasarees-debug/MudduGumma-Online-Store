Shipping Policy

Clear information about order processing, delivery estimates, charges, tracking and what to do when a parcel needs help.

Effective 31 August 2026
On this pageDelivery coverageProcessingDelivery estimatesChargesTrackingAddress changesDelivery issuesContact us

We currently ship orders to serviceable addresses in India. Orders are normally prepared within 1–3 business days, followed by an estimated courier delivery time of 3–10 business days.

1. Delivery coverage

Shipping is available to Indian PIN codes supported by our courier partners. Serviceability can depend on the product, destination and courier network. International shipping is not available unless it is specifically offered and confirmed before you place the order.

2. Order processing

We normally process confirmed orders within 1–3 business days. Sundays, public holidays, payment verification, high-volume sale periods, made-to-order work or requested customisation may add time.

Processing begins after payment confirmation and successful order checks. If an item becomes unavailable, we will contact you and provide an appropriate replacement, revised timeline or refund option.

3. Estimated delivery time

After dispatch, delivery is generally estimated within 3–10 business days, depending on the destination and courier service. Remote or restricted locations may take longer.

Please note: Delivery dates are estimates, not guarantees. Courier delays, weather, public events, strikes, natural events, address problems or other circumstances outside reasonable control can affect them.

4. Shipping charges

Any delivery charge, free-shipping offer or location-based fee will be shown before you complete payment. You will not be charged a new shipping amount after payment unless you separately request and agree to a change in delivery service or destination.

5. Dispatch and tracking

When tracking is available, it may be sent through the order page, email, SMS or WhatsApp details provided with the order. Courier tracking can take up to 24–48 hours after dispatch to show movement.

6. Delivery address and changes

  • Please provide a complete address, correct PIN code and reachable mobile number.
  • Contact us immediately if an address needs correction. We can change it only before dispatch and only where the courier permits.
  • We are not responsible for delay or failed delivery caused by incorrect or incomplete customer-provided information.
  • If a parcel is returned because delivery was refused or repeatedly unsuccessful, re-shipping charges may apply. We will explain the available options before taking action.

7. Delayed, damaged, missing or incorrect parcels

If tracking is unusually delayed, contact us and we will raise an enquiry with the courier. If the parcel appears lost, we will investigate and offer replacement or refund as appropriate after courier confirmation.

If a parcel arrives visibly damaged, wrong or incomplete, preserve the packaging and contact us promptly with the order number and clear photographs. Please also see our Cancellation & Refund Policy.
